Data deletion

CCS Publisher, last updated 27 October 2026

CCS Publisher is a private publishing tool used only by its owner, to post videos to the owner's own Facebook Pages and Instagram account. It does not let anyone else sign in, and it does not collect or store data about other people.

What CCS Publisher keeps

Only the access tokens for the owner's own accounts, stored on the owner's computer, plus statistics about the owner's own posts. Nothing is sold or shared.

How to remove CCS Publisher and its data

  1. Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → select CCS Publisher → Remove. Facebook then revokes its access.
  2. Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → CCS Publisher → Remove.
  3. To ask for any remaining data to be deleted, send a message to the Cricket Captain Showdowns Facebook Page. Requests are handled within 30 days, and we confirm when the data is gone.
