CCS Publisher

The publishing tool behind Cricket Captain Showdowns

What it is

Cricket Captain Showdowns makes short, original animated videos about cricket: the physics, the records and the moments that shouldn't have happened. CCS Publisher is the channel's own private tool. It uploads each finished video to the channel's YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts and reads their performance statistics.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner, on their own computer, for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other people.
