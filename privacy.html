Privacy policy

CCS Publisher, last updated 24 October 2026

CCS Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Cricket Captain Showdowns channel to publish the channel's own videos to its own accounts. It does not collect data from, or sign in, anyone else.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (Google sign-in): uploading and scheduling the channel's videos, editing their titles, descriptions and captions, posting comments, and reading the channel's statistics and YouTube Analytics reports.
  • Google Drive (only files the tool itself creates): a copy of a finished video is stored briefly so Instagram can fetch it, then deleted. The tool cannot see any other Drive files.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta sign-in): publishing Reels to the channel's own Instagram account and Facebook Page, managing comments on them, and reading their insights.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens and statistics stay on the owner's computer. They are used only to publish the channel's videos and to measure how they perform. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CCS Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google account permissions and from Meta's Business Integrations / Apps and Websites settings. Deleting the local token files ends access immediately.

Contact

Message the channel through its Facebook Page or YouTube channel.
